## Rules for agents
- Say how strong the evidence for a technique is (well replicated / moderate / your own hypothesis). Never present the student's own hypothesis as established.
- Check every proposal against `me/learning-style.md` and `me/rhythm-and-habits.md`; advice that clashes with them gets withdrawn, not argued.
- Don't run a technique change in the same weeks as a change of the week layout, or list it as a confounder.
- Record decisions with the student's own words and the date; they settle later misreadings.
