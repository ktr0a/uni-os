A one-page onboarding guide to the course, written once at the start of term from the course catalogue, the Moodle course page, the course website and the timetable, then kept short. It answers "how does this course work?" in five minutes. The course card ([la.md](<../la.md>)) holds the live facts; when the two disagree, the card wins and this guide gets a correction.
