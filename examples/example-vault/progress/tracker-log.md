# Progress tracker log

Source of truth for the history of progress: every status message, result, decision and plan change, one dated line each. The live summary is [current-status.md](current-status.md); rules for agents are in [progress-tracking](../_agent/progress-tracking.md).

- **Append only.** Never rewrite or delete a line. Fix a wrong entry by appending a correction line.
- **User statements win over derived data.** Update on every status message; the check-in reads this log.
- Term week 0 starts Mon 2026-09-14; week n starts week 0 + 7n days. Times are local (Europe/Zurich).
- Items marked **(?)** are open questions to Alex Example. Unconfirmed dictated items say "to be confirmed".

Line format: `- <Ddd D Mon YYYY> ~HH:MM (<source>): **<key fact>** <details>; <consequence>; <what is unknown>.`
