# Exam-phase plan: lecture-free period and exam session

A frame, not a calendar. Plan & progress turns it into real blocks week by week and fills in the exam dates once the university publishes them. It only **schedules** study time; what you practise in each block (your backlog, your error logs, your summary sheets, practice material your course provides) is decided with your course sessions.

## House rules (the same as during the term unless changed here)
- Start at 08:00; no study blocks after 19:00.
- Blocks of about 70 minutes, then a break. At most 5 blocks on a normal day, 3 to 4 on a travel or away day.
- One full rest day per week; named rest days around holidays.
- Each exam day ends work for that day, and the next morning is off.
- Graded solo work and your own summary sheets stay yours: agents check wording against the script, they don't write them.

## The material (what the blocks draw on)
- **Backlogs:** each course's `backlog.md` lists the tasks parked during the term ("for later"). They are the warm-up material.
- **Error logs:** during the term, reread them weekly; in the exam phase, one sweep per week and a full pass through a course's log two days before its exam.
- **Summary sheets** (if a course allows aids): half a page per week from mid-term, a full draft by the start of the lecture-free period, revised after each timed practice, frozen a few days before the exam (print two copies if printed). Without aids: your own one-page summary of definitions and formulas, recalled from memory each day in the last week.
- **Practice material:** whatever you and your course sessions choose (unfinished sheets from your backlog, the course's own practice sets). This plan only schedules the blocks; what goes into them is decided in the course sessions.

## Phases

### Phase 0: wind-down (the first days after the last lectures)
- Submit the online items still due before any travel.
- 2 to 3 blocks a day at most: backlog, error-log sweep, summary-sheet pages. Rest days around holidays.
- Set up what the later phases need (offline copies, the software setup for computer-based exams).

### Phase 1: topic review (3 to 4 blocks a day)
- Work through each course's topics, one course per block, rotating; the lowest self-rated course gets extra blocks (rebalance rule).
- One backlog task per course per day as a warm-up.
- Summary sheets: complete the drafts.

### Phase 2: timed practice and repair (5 blocks a day)
- One full-length timed practice per course per week under exam conditions (with your summary sheet if aids are allowed), then correction: mark, write error-log rows, add what was missing to the summary sheet.
- The other days: repair the topics the practice showed.

Day template (Plan & progress sets the real times):

| Slot | Practice day | Topic day |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | warm-up: summary-sheet recall + 3 error-log rows | course A topic |
| 2-3 | full timed practice (the real exam length) | course A, then course B topic |
| 4 | correction: mark, error-log rows | course B topic |
| 5 | correction done, summary-sheet "to add" list | course C, backlog task |

### Phase 3: exam session, countdown per exam (T = exam day)

| Day | Plan |
|---|---|
| T-10 to T-5 | this course gets 3 of 5 blocks; others get 1 to 2 maintenance blocks; one more full timed practice |
| T-4 | final timed practice under real conditions |
| T-3 | correction; repair only what it showed; freeze and print the summary sheet if printed |
| T-2 | full error-log pass for this course; summary-sheet read-through; key definitions and formulas from memory |
| T-1 | light: 2 to 3 blocks, nothing new; logistics (room, ID, pens, allowed aids in the bag); stop by 17:00 |
| T | exam; no study after it |
| T+1 | morning off; afternoon: switch to the next course's countdown |

**Merge rule:** if two exams are 3 days or less apart, the earlier exam owns everything until it is written; the later one gets only its T-2 and T-1, so its final timed practice moves before the earlier exam.

**Course order before the dates are known:** weight goes to the first exam; within that, the lowest self-rated courses get extra blocks.

## Who watches for the published exam dates
- Owner: Plan & progress.
- Trigger: each check-in in the last five teaching weeks asks one line, "Are your exam dates out yet?", until you paste or screenshot them (the original started asking about five weeks before the last lecture). Personal exam plans usually sit behind the university login, so an agent can't fetch them unattended (a read-only browser check is possible if you ask). An automatic watch of the cloud folder was considered and rejected in the original: nothing about the exam plan ever lands there.
- When the dates arrive: fill the `[EXAM ...: TBD]` placeholders above, book the countdowns, extend the study-block template, and tell the daily-prep routine (nothing to do: it reads the calendar).
